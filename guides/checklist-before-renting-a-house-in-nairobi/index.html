Checklist: 20 things to check before renting a house in Nairobi

TrueHome Kenya · Updated 23 October 2026

Take this checklist to every viewing. It covers the things Kenyan tenants most often regret not asking about.

Water and power

  • Is water constant? Is there a borehole or a storage tank, and how big?
  • Is the water bill included in rent, or separate? How is it measured?
  • Prepaid token meter or postpaid? Is there a separate meter for your unit?
  • Is there backup power for the lift, gate and security lights?

Security

  • Is the compound gated, with a guard at night (or 24 hours)?
  • CCTV, electric fence, good lighting on the way in?
  • Walk the route to the main road or matatu stage, ideally after dark.

The house itself

  • Turn on taps and the shower: pressure and hot water?
  • Look for damp, cracks and leaks on ceilings and under sinks.
  • Check windows lock, doors close, and there is mobile signal in every room.
  • Fibre internet available? Which providers?
  • Parking: how many slots, and are they allocated?

Money and paperwork

  • How many months' deposit, and when exactly is it refunded?
  • Any service charge, garbage or security fees on top of rent?
  • Get a written tenancy agreement: notice period, rent increases, repairs, who pays what.
  • Who is the landlord? Ask for their name and ID, and confirm the agent or caretaker is authorised.
  • Pay into a named account and keep every receipt.
  • Take dated photos of the house's condition on moving day.

Neighbourhood

  • Distance to work, schools, shops and hospital at rush hour.
  • Ask a current tenant what they like and dislike about the building.
  • Noise: bars, churches, construction, main roads.
